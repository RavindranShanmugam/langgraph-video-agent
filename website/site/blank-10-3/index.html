0–1 Designer, UX & Human-AI Interaction Consultant, Entrepreneurial Researcher

Human-first. Design-led. Tech-enabled.

Passionately crafting responsible solutions, from vision to reality for 7+ years. I am fluent in Research based design

I believe impactful design quietly empowers, not shouts. My philosophy is to get the basics right, understand deeply, design meaningfully, and execute purposefully.

 

Entrepreneurship fuels my creativity, from ideation brainstorms to turning ambitious hackathon concepts into investor-backed ventures.

 

Driven by curiosity, I experiment, transforming concepts into rigorous research, prototyping rapidly, and validating solutions through user insights. Each design challenge is an opportunity to create clear, intuitive, and human interfaces.

 

Great design is experiential, empathetic, and thoughtful. My focus is purposeful simplicity—clarity over clutter, function over fluff, solutions over aesthetics.

 

Artistic expression, through painting, motion graphics, and video editing, enriches my design practice. My love for technology keeps me exploring, with Generative AI as my latest favorite playground.
